# Tickr backend audit and offline-launch plan

**Date:** 2026-10-07  
**Status:** Implementation approved on 2026-10-07 (“yes process”). P01 implemented and validated locally; P02–P33 remain pending. No production rollout authorized or performed.  
**Launch:** Tunisia, French, reserve online → pay organizer/seller → seller records cash → ticket becomes admissible. Online gateways remain in the repository but are disabled. “Offline payment” means cash outside Tickr, **not disconnected scanning**.

## Decision record — 2026-10-07 follow-up

The user approved reuse of existing states/rules, zero commission, and confirmed that no tickets have been distributed. They delegated commission, door, permissions, SMS, quotas and onboarding choices. The subsequent “yes process” authorizes implementation using these selections, including the recommended timing/refund/delivery defaults. They are **not implemented capabilities** unless marked complete below. The source audit remains a historical preimplementation baseline; the progress record identifies subsequent changes.

| Decision | Selected launch policy | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C01 — States | Reuse existing order/ticket statuses. Add `OFFLINE` payment method and typed reasons, not redundant status enums. | User approved. |
| C02 — Commission | 0% for every offline order, regardless of online event overrides; no minimum fee or gateway fee. Reject nonzero offline commission configuration in the launch profile. Preserve online configuration and snapshot zero fee/receivable; nonzero receivables require a later separately approved pricing release. | User preference + delegated recommendation. |
| C03 — Physical cash/refunds | Seller receives/returns physical cash; Tickr records attestations, never moves money. Record the exact order amount, with change handled outside Tickr; no deposits or partial payments. Recommend full-order manual refunds for V1, owner-only. “Used” means already scanned for entry, not paid. A refund after entry needs an explicit owner exception/reason, preserves the check-in history and never replenishes consumed stock. Partial-ticket/partial-amount refunds are deferred. | Physical cash confirmed; refund scope is a recommendation clarifying the user's question. |
| C04 — Door | One short code per order, one signed QR per ticket. Default action is collection only. Explicit “Encaisser + Entrée” requires both permissions and one selected ticket; collection plus that entry is one database transaction. Failure rolls back both database changes; UI must clearly show that physical cash may still need returning or recording with collection-only after refreshing state. Never mark the whole group as entered. | Delegated recommendation. |
| C05 — Permissions | CHECKIN scans; COLLECT_PAYMENT looks up and collects but cannot refund, cancel another person's hold, export or view totals. Both permit combined action. Purchaser can cancel their own unpaid hold; event owner can cancel any unpaid hold and record refunds. Admin may cancel for support with audited reason, but no routine cash collection/refund or cash CSV access merely from ADMIN role. Cash totals/CSV remain event-owner-only. Existing admin check-in support remains. | Delegated least-privilege recommendation; no new global role. |
| C06 — Timing | Recommend 24h hold, configurable 1–72h, still capped at start−2h. Door collection ends at min(start+30min, event end), grace configurable 0–120min. No new reservations at/after event start. One reminder 2h before expiry, configurable 15min–24h; skip if reminder time precedes creation or delivery would be after expiry. | Unanswered question: recommended defaults, not user-confirmed. |
| C07 — SMS/OTP | Reuse Amazon SNS behind the existing SMS port as the initial candidate; validate Tunisia delivery/cost before release. User-requested security OTP may send 24/7 subject to local/provider rules; ordinary notifications remain 08–20 Tunis. Use a real branded STOP/unsubscribe link, not a promise of SMS replies to an alphanumeric sender. Provider qualification, operational budget and legal checks remain release gates. | Delegated recommendation, with external prerequisites. |
| C08 — Offline limits | At most **4 outstanding unpaid offline tickets per purchaser/event**, summed across all active orders, and **3 active unpaid offline orders across events**. Paid/checked-in tickets do not consume the hold quota; neither do cancelled/expired/refunded orders. Online orders retain their own policy. This limits unpaid stock hoarding, not lifetime purchases. Rate-limit successful new offline reservations to 5/account/hour, including cancelled/recreated ones; retries replay rather than consume a new slot. | Delegated recommendation; supersedes initial held+paid counting proposal. |
| C09 — QR | Issue/accept HMAC v2 for launch. No production legacy-v1 acceptance or migration period needed because no tickets have been distributed. Keep old fixtures/parser only for explicit internal regression purposes; never expose a public v1 downgrade. Test-data resets must be opt-in and nonproduction only. | User confirms prelaunch/no distributed tickets. |
| C10 — Organizers | Self-service profile creation with verified email + current phone, profile/contact completeness and seller-terms acceptance before publishing/collecting. No default manual approval queue; rate limits, reporting and admin suspension provide abuse controls. Contact verification is not legal identity/KYC certification. | Delegated recommendation. |
| C11 — Existing rules | Retain 10 types, ≥1h advance creation, immutable published dates/location, UUID APIs and current pagination. No zero-price event or disconnected scanner work in this launch. Keep email login; phone OTP does not imply adding phone login. | User agrees to keep existing rules; deferred-scope recommendation. |
| C12 — Delivery/operations | Recommend email with a secure paid-ticket download link and account access, not mandatory attachments. Reservation email/page shows QR/code but no final PDF. Organizer handles actual cash/refund disputes; Tickr provides audit/support, not settlement. Turning off new offline reservations honors existing holds until their snapshotted deadlines; a separate audited emergency control can stop collections. Retention/legal terms and named operational owners still need launch sign-off. | Recommended interpretation of the ambiguous final “yes”. |

**SMS evidence checked:** [AWS country support](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/phone-numbers-sms-by-country.html) lists Tunisia with sender IDs/international sending, but no two-way SMS or local short/long codes. [SNS opt-out documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sns/latest/dg/sms_manage.html) describes reply-based opt-out to an origination number; it is not evidence that replies to a Tunisian sender ID work. A functioning web opt-out must be validated against local/provider requirements; if reply STOP is mandatory, qualify a local two-way provider through the same port before launch. SNS is the lowest-change candidate, not a claim of cheapest or best-tested Tunisian delivery.

## 1. Scope, evidence and verdict

### Implementation progress — P01

- **Done locally:** strict `PAYMENT_GATEWAYS_ENABLED=false` and `OFFLINE_PAYMENT_ENABLED=true` defaults in [payment configuration](../backend/src/config/payments.config.ts); validated values are exactly `true`/`false`. The offline flag is policy only, **not a completed offline payment method**. No enum/provider/commission/reservation implementation is exposed yet.
- [Gateway policy](../backend/src/modules/payments/infrastructure/config/payment-gateway.policy.ts) gates factory access and every direct adapter intent/confirmation/refund call. Disabled Stripe skips SDK initialization and credential requirements. Flags are deployment configuration; restart after changing them, not an in-flight runtime kill switch.
- Disabled creation/payment/refund commands return before reservation, intent persistence, URL replay or refund mutations. Webhooks return HTTP 403 `PAYMENT_METHOD_DISABLED` before verification, dedupe, network or success/failure handlers. Existing routes remain. [Public configuration](../backend/src/modules/payments/infrastructure/controllers/public-config.controller.ts) adds `availablePaymentMethods`, empty when gateways are disabled; it never advertises unfinished OFFLINE. Existing pricing fields remain legacy online pricing, not a claim that offline commission has shipped.
- [Global exception filter](../backend/src/shared/infrastructure/common/filters/all-exceptions.filter.ts) preserves explicit machine codes, falling back to existing HTTP error labels. No schema migration, dependency installation, gateway/SMS call, production deployment or commit was performed.
- **Validation:** 32 focused suites / 344 tests passed (payments unit tests, in-memory payments integration, configuration, isolated HTTP contracts and shared filter); 34 architecture tests passed; backend build and `tsc --noEmit` passed; lint has 0 errors and the same 4 pre-existing Money VO warnings; `git diff --check` passed. Editor diagnostics on new files are clear. Architecture initially caught a helper in the adapter directory; it was moved to infrastructure configuration and the suite passed on rerun.
- **Limits:** HTTP contracts boot real controllers/factory/adapters without credentials but mock commands and auth; separate command tests prove disabled side-effect suppression. This is not full AppModule/database boot or real-provider validation. Broad all-backend regression attempts returned unusable terminal output, so no new all-backend pass claim is made. Existing gateway correctness/security and ticket bypass debt remain P06/P07/R04 work; do not re-enable gateways for production based on these tests.
- **Next:** P02 repairs test discovery/E2E fixture providers before database foundation work. No live SMS configuration or spending was enabled; provider/budget/retention gates below remain.

The product targets are the [functional overview](01-fonctionnel/01-vue-ensemble.md#L1), [20 detailed user stories](01-fonctionnel/02-specifications-detaillees.md#L1), [business rules](01-fonctionnel/03-regles-metier.md#L1), and the offline-payment request supplied for this plan. Current source determines what exists; conflicts are recorded below, not silently resolved.

**Verdict:** reuse the six bounded contexts, existing order/pricing aggregate, ticket model, event staff assignments, PDF generator, SES/SNS adapters and check-in transaction. Offline commerce itself is missing. It is not just a fourth gateway adapter: stock/order/ticket atomicity, phone verification, collection permissions, cash auditing and deadline coordination are required before launch.

The [September audit](10-project-audit/00-executive-report.md#L1) is useful historical context, not proof that every old finding is still current. Examples directly rechecked here:

- The [payment ticket adapter](../backend/src/modules/payments/infrastructure/adapters/ticket-reservation.adapter.ts#L16) is still a stub. [Order creation](../backend/src/modules/payments/application/commands/create-order/create-order.handler.ts#L129) ignores returned reservation IDs. [Confirmation](../backend/src/modules/payments/application/commands/confirm-payment/confirm-payment.handler.ts#L66) uses order-item IDs as ticket IDs.
- Registration still [does not mint or send verification tokens](../backend/src/modules/users/infrastructure/controllers/auth.controller.ts#L155), while login requires verified email. Password reset creates a token but does not send it.
- The actual ticket [QR format uses MD5 checksum, not HMAC](../backend/src/modules/tickets/domain/value-objects/qr-code.vo.ts#L1). No existing ticket HMAC signer was found in the inspected source. The image renderer is not a signer.
- The [PDF generator exists](../backend/src/modules/tickets/infrastructure/services/pdf-generator.service.ts#L1); do not rebuild it. Its [confirmation listener](../backend/src/modules/tickets/infrastructure/event-handlers/ticket-confirmed-infra.listener.ts#L55) still supplies a placeholder event title and empty location and lacks notification handoff.
- [Analytics controller identity](../backend/src/modules/analytics/infrastructure/controllers/analytics.controller.ts#L69) currently uses `user.userId`; the old claim that this controller uses `id` should not be repeated. Nevertheless, [event analytics authorization is not enforced in the handler](../backend/src/modules/analytics/application/queries/get-event-analytics/get-event-analytics.handler.ts#L29), including on cache hits.
- [Notification templates already have a migration](../backend/src/shared/infrastructure/database/migrations/1700000000000005-create-notifications-tables.ts#L90). Missing template content/wiring must not be confused with a missing template table.

### 4.3 Payment abstraction, flags and pricing

Reuse [PaymentProviderPort/factory](../backend/src/modules/payments/application/ports/payment-provider.port.ts#L23) and [provider factory adapter](../backend/src/modules/payments/infrastructure/adapters/payment-provider-factory.adapter.ts#L1). Add an `OfflinePaymentProvider` implementation behind that factory with **no network calls**. An offline intent means “await seller collection”, not automatic successful payment. Generic `confirmPayment(referenceId)` cannot prove receipt of cash; it must not provide a shortcut around the authorized collection command. `verifyWebhook` for OFFLINE must not accept anything; manual refunds are recorded by a dedicated authorized use case, not inferred from a no-op provider result.

The existing abstraction has gateway-shaped return types. Extend it compatibly with explicit capabilities/optional offline instructions, rather than fake gateway transaction references or force unpaid cash orders into PROCESSING. Keep one order creation pipeline and one Money/pricing calculation; method-specific policy supplies deadlines/rate/intent behavior.

Flags parsed and validated server-side:

- `PAYMENT_GATEWAYS_ENABLED=false`: excludes Stripe/Konnect/Paymee from discovery/public config; guards direct `/orders/:id/pay`, factory access, callback mutation and all alternative entry points. Existing paths remain, returning a documented disabled-method response. No gateway credentials or external calls required to boot the offline profile. Retain gateway code and regression tests under a separate enabled test profile.
- `OFFLINE_PAYMENT_ENABLED=true`: OFFLINE is offered only when global flag **and** event opt-in are true, and event/ticket sale eligibility permits reservation. Per-user OTP/quota status should be returned through authenticated eligibility responses, not leak into shared public cache.
- Both flags false → empty methods and no new purchase. Turning OFFLINE off stops new reservations; existing holds remain collectible until their original deadline, and cancellation/refund stay available. A separate audited operational emergency stop can halt collections.

Selected pricing: OFFLINE uses `OFFLINE_COMMISSION_RATE=0`, never the online 6% event/global override or a minimum fee. Validate the launch profile against nonzero offline rates; keep online overrides unchanged for future gateway use. Snapshot effective rate/source, face subtotal, zero fee, total and zero receivable; `paymentFees=0`. A future nonzero offline commission would be a **receivable only**, never a Tickr settlement. Its payer/recognition/reversal policy is deferred to a separate approved release, not a blocker for the zero-fee launch.

### 4.4 Deadline and inventory rules

- `PAY_BEFORE_DEADLINE`: `deadline = min(createdAt + holdHours, eventStart - 2h)`.
- Recommended `holdHours=24`, configurable 1–72h. `PAY_AT_DOOR`: `deadline = min(eventStart + configured grace, eventEnd)`; recommended grace 30min, configurable 0–120min. New reservations require `now < eventStart` and the existing ticket sales window; only earlier holds can be collected after start. Reject impossible/past deadlines before allocating stock.
- Recommended one reminder at deadline−2h, configurable 15min–24h. Skip if that instant is at/before creation; defer ordinary SMS to the next allowed time only if still useful before expiry. Do not send stale reminders or silently extend holds.
- Use the same trusted clock and persisted instant for the order and every ticket. Display in `Africa/Tunis`; store UTC instants. Do not recompute deadlines from later settings edits.
- Collection checks deadline **in the database transaction** even if the scheduler has not run. Specify boundary as `now < deadline`; expiry at `now >= deadline`, and align entity methods accordingly.
- Reject mismatched event/ticket type, mixed currencies, non-integer or nonpositive quantities, duplicate-line quota bypass, and holders count different from quantity. Current [creation validation](../backend/src/modules/payments/application/commands/create-order/create-order.handler.ts#L75) does not provide all these guarantees.
- Reserve stock exactly once; mark-paid changes held inventory into sold entitlement, not a second decrement. Cancellation/expiry releases exactly once. Existing `soldQuantity` is used for held availability: separate reservation counts in reporting or migrate stock representation deliberately.
- Both [ticket expiry handler](../backend/src/modules/tickets/application/commands/expire-tickets/expire-tickets.handler.ts#L100) and [expiry listener](../backend/src/modules/tickets/infrastructure/event-handlers/ticket-expired-infra.listener.ts#L36) release availability; cancellation has the same duplicate responsibility in [handler](../backend/src/modules/tickets/application/commands/cancel-tickets/cancel-tickets.handler.ts#L106) and [listener](../backend/src/modules/tickets/infrastructure/event-handlers/ticket-cancelled-infra.listener.ts#L37). Consolidate, do not layer another release on top.

### 4.5 Atomicity, idempotency and module boundaries

Introduce an application-owned commerce transaction port with plain domain/application input/output types. Its PostgreSQL implementation belongs in an **infrastructure adapter**, the permitted integration boundary. Use one `DataSource` transaction/connection across module-owned tables, via transaction-scoped persistence adapters. Never expose TypeORM `EntityManager` in domain/application contracts, and never call an HTTP endpoint from another module to simulate atomicity.

Transaction boundaries:

1. **Reserve:** serialize per-user eligibility/quota; validate live event/method/phone; lock affected stock rows in deterministic order; conditionally allocate stock; persist order + real linked tickets + reservation audit + outbox. Roll back everything on failure. An order/item ID is never a substitute for ticket ID. Creation idempotency prevents retry/network duplication and is scoped to actor+request payload.
2. **Mark paid:** verify live actor/assignment permission, method, state and deadline; lock order and ticket rows; conditionally transition unpaid order; insert unique cash collection record; confirm all reserved tickets; add transitions/outbox. Validate received amount before mutation. No external I/O inside transaction.
3. **Concurrent sellers:** one commit; loser receives `ALREADY_PAID` with permitted collector identity/time. Reauthorize before returning those details. Same-key retry replays result without another audit/charge/entry; differing payload under same key is conflict. A stale browser must not overwrite the first collector.
4. **Collect + entry:** require both CHECKIN and COLLECT_PAYMENT (owner implicit; ADMIN alone does not authorize cash collection); confirm whole order but check in only an explicitly selected/scanned ticket. Reuse transaction-scoped check-in persistence, not the public handler which opens its own transaction. Audit both transitions. All-or-nothing database commit if selected ticket cannot be admitted. Physical cash cannot be rolled back by software: after failure the seller refreshes authoritative state, then returns cash or deliberately records collection-only if still eligible. Never silently retry as collection-only.
5. **Expire / cancel / refund:** contend on the same order/ticket state locks; mutate stock and audit in that transaction. Workers claim batches with DB locks/leases; process small bounded transactions. Expiry cannot undo paid state, and a second worker cannot issue duplicate strikes/notifications/releases.
6. **Outbox:** business transaction commits event records. Consumers create PDF/send notifications/update projections afterward with idempotency and retry. Consumer failure never reports a paid order as unpaid or releases paid inventory. Existing in-memory event storage is not a durable outbox.

This is a modular-monolith transaction design, **not an eventual-consistency saga as a substitute for required atomic stock updates**. Define lock order, bounded deadlock retries, and concurrent assignment-revocation semantics in tests.

### 4.6 QR, lookup and admission

- Use a signed `v2` ticket payload with version/key ID, event ID, ticket ID and random nonce, HMAC-SHA256 over canonical serialization. Freeze the exact byte format with test vectors in P16. Include neither PII nor payment status. Use a dedicated secret/key lifecycle, constant-time verification and version rejection; keep keys outside logs/repository.
- No distributed tickets exist. Launch issuance and public verification require v2; reject v1/MD5 instead of adding a production grace period. Fail closed without a signing key. Keep any legacy fixtures isolated to internal tests; never delete persisted test/development data without an explicit environment-safe operation.
- Proposed human code: one cryptographically random, normalized `TKR-…` code per order with database uniqueness and collision retry. It is a locator, not a payment/admission credential. Bound lookup attempts by actor/event/IP; avoid global enumeration and distinguish neither cross-event nor unauthorized orders to callers.
- QR lookup resolves a ticket to its order and event; short-code lookup returns that order with selectable ticket references. Response is minimized: holder name, ticket types/quantities, amount/currency, state, deadline and allowed actions. No holder contact details, full QR list, payment secrets, other-event data, totals or exports for staff.
- Unpaid scan returns machine code `UNPAID`, French message, amount due and `canCollect` derived from current permission. It never silently marks entry or payment. Unauthorized event scanners receive no amount/data. Existing paid tickets follow the current event match, time window and duplicate-check-in rules.
- Denied/invalid scans receive security audit records without storing raw QR/OTP payloads. Successful check-in audit includes actor/device/IP/time and assignment provenance.

### 4.7 OTP, limits and no-shows

- Require verified **current** phone before every offline reservation eligibility check; the first reservation triggers verification if needed. Persist verification to avoid asking for an OTP on every purchase. Changing phone invalidates it. Do not trust a boolean in a JWT or request body.
- Purpose-bound, hashed, short-lived, single-use OTP challenges. Configure expiry, attempts, resend cooldown, per-phone/account/IP send limits, aggregate SMS budget and abuse alerts. Never log or return OTP; throttle both request and verification. Secure challenge consumption and verification write in a transaction.
- Offline defaults: at most **4 outstanding unpaid tickets per purchaser/event** across active OFFLINE orders, and **3 active unpaid offline orders/user** across events. Active means PENDING+OFFLINE with `now < deadline`. Paid/checked-in/cancelled/refunded/expired and online orders do not consume this hold quota. No lifetime offline purchase cap is introduced. Purchaser owns quotas, not holder email; enforce across lines and concurrent requests. Expired-but-unprocessed rows do not count toward eligibility, but their stock is reusable only after transactional expiry/release.
- Persist a creation-rate budget of 5 successful new offline reservations/account/rolling hour, including subsequently cancelled orders, to prevent cancel/recreate hoarding and SMS abuse. Idempotent retries do not count again. Combine account rules with phone uniqueness, lookup/IP throttling and monitoring; do not claim this fully prevents multiple-account abuse.
- Each system-expired unpaid order records one no-show strike, not one per ticket. Three in a rolling 90-day interval yields a 30-day block. All values configurable. Seller/event cancellation and manual refunds do not count as no-shows.
- Admin lift records actor/reason/time and marks existing strikes forgiven for future threshold calculations without deleting them. A completed 30-day block likewise consumes its triggering strikes so the same set cannot reblock immediately. Only new, unforgiven, unconsumed strikes count toward another block. Confirmed platform-outage intervals suppress/forgive associated strikes through an audited correction; expiry still releases stock and never auto-extends deadlines.

OTP defaults: cryptographically random 6 digits, 5min expiry, 5 verification attempts, 60s resend cooldown, 3 sends/15min and 5/day per account **and** normalized phone. Resending invalidates the prior code without resetting account/phone attempt budgets. Store a keyed digest with a secret outside the database (plain fast hashes of six digits are not enough against offline enumeration). Add configurable IP/global limits and an atomic application send budget; provider billing limits alone are not a guaranteed hard cap. Security OTP is user-requested and 24/7 only where lawful; do not bypass provider suppression or silently opt a number back in. Ordinary notification opt-out is separate from explicit OTP requests. No email fallback may falsely mark a phone verified.

### 4.10 Refund and cash reconciliation semantics

- Unpaid cancellation is not a refund; no cash audit entry pretending money moved.
- For paid OFFLINE orders, authorized organizer acknowledges cash returned; save refund amount/currency, `refundedBy`, `refundedAt`, reason, affected ticket IDs and original collection reference. No Stripe/Konnect/Paymee call, no automated settlement.
- Recommended V1: owner records the full order amount only **after physically returning it**, with explicit acknowledgement/reason and idempotency. Participant requests are not proof of cash return; staff/admin status alone cannot attest organizer refunds. A checked-in ticket means someone entered. If any ticket was used, require an explicit owner exception and preserve `CHECKED_IN` plus refund evidence; cancel all still-unused tickets, set order REFUNDED, and deny subsequent admission. Never erase entry history or replenish consumed stock. Unused refunded stock can be released once, without reopening closed/cancelled-event sales. Partial-order/partial-amount refunds are deferred; do not misrecord a partial physical refund as full. Deposits are unsupported; seller handles cash change physically, while `amountReceived` records only the exact net order total.
- Per-collector reconciliation uses immutable successful collection rows, not current order status alone. Show gross cash collected, manual refunds separately and net cash; original collector and refunding actor may differ. Count collected orders and ticket quantities distinctly. CSV totals/list must match the same filters/snapshot and use TND millime precision.
- Revenue excludes unpaid/expired reservations; paid sales and refund adjustments are separate measures. Outstanding commission receivable is not collected platform revenue. Staff cannot infer event totals via unrestricted pagination/count endpoints.

## 7. Risks and open questions

### Main risks

- **False atomicity:** chaining existing handlers or asynchronous events is not one transaction. Releasing stock in both listeners and handlers is a concrete duplication risk to remove.
- **Legacy bypass:** `/tickets/confirm`, cancellation, detail/PDF/transfer routes and generic provider confirmation must not bypass cash collection/phone quotas.
- **Cash disagreement:** backend records an attestation of receipt, not bank verification. Require clear confirmation UX, immutable collector identity and manual correction/refund policy.
- **Deadline ambiguity:** before-start cutoff, after-start door grace, ticket sales windows, event completion and SMS quiet hours interact. Test boundaries before accepting long holds.
- **Short-code enumeration/QR copying:** use scoped authenticated lookup, throttling and single online redemption. HMAC protects authenticity, not screenshots or a stolen bearer token.
- **SMS cost/availability:** free commission is not zero operating cost; OTP abuse and Tunisia sender/STOP support need an actual provider plan. French Unicode can multiply billed segments.
- **PII/audit retention:** trusted IP/device evidence is sensitive; minimize/redact logs, restrict exports and define retention before launch.
- **Schema drift:** development synchronize can hide absent migrations. Do not trust unit tests or ORM class presence as deployment proof.
- **Mixed audit/history:** already confirmed/refunded legacy tickets and ambiguous historical expiry cannot always be backfilled perfectly. Preserve unknown provenance instead of inventing collector identities.
- **Receivable ambiguity:** nonzero offline fees can misrepresent what Tickr earned or who owes it. Keep zero until explicitly resolved; no settlement implementation in this scope.
- **Infrastructure uncertainty:** no production deployment, penetration/load test, SMS contract, legal review or delivered-message SLA was verified in this plan.
